Privacy Policy for Sloth
Last Updated: August 29, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Discord bot Sloth ("the Bot") processes, for what purpose, and what rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data Controller
The party responsible for data processing (Data Controller) is:

Developer: GamingToasti Contact Email: slothbotdev@gmail.com Discord: gamingtoasti

2. Data We Collect
The Bot processes data solely to provide its services. This includes:

Discord User Data: User ID, Username, Discriminator, Avatar URL.

Role Assignments: If the server uses the timed roles or role events features, the Bot stores a snapshot of the role IDs currently assigned to you on that server. This snapshot exists solely to detect role changes and to restore or remove temporary roles when they expire. It is obtained through the Server Members privileged intent.

Guild (Server) Data: Server ID, Channel IDs, Role IDs.

Interaction Data: Commands used, timestamps, and parameters provided by the user.

Technical Logs: Error messages and system events for debugging.

Message Content Policy: The Bot does not store message content (the text you write) in any database. Message content is processed briefly in the system's working memory (RAM) strictly to execute commands or filter content (if moderation modules are enabled) and is discarded immediately afterwards.

3. Purpose of Processing
We process your data for the following purposes:

Service Provision: To execute the commands you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Moderation & Management: To store warnings, bans, or configuration settings as requested by server administrators (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Security & Stability: To detect bugs, prevent abuse, and ensure the Bot runs smoothly (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR - Legitimate Interest).

4. Data Storage and Retention
We store data only as long as necessary:

Configuration Data: Stored as long as the Bot is on your server. If the Bot is kicked or removed, all data belonging to that server is automatically deleted 30 days later. This deletion is performed by an automated daily job; no manual request is required. If the Bot is added back within those 30 days, the deletion is cancelled.

Moderation Logs (Warns/Bans): Stored persistently to maintain server history until a server administrator requests deletion or resets the data, or until the server's retention period above expires.

Role Snapshots: Deleted automatically as soon as you leave the server they belong to.

User Profiles: The stored username and avatar URL are deleted automatically once no warning, ticket, reminder, role snapshot, leveling entry or statistic refers to your User ID any more.

Technical Logs: Error messages and system events are written to the host's log files and are rotated automatically after 14 days. They are not stored in the database.

5. Data Sharing and Hosting
We do not sell or trade your data. However, to operate the Bot, we use technical infrastructure:

Discord API: All data is transmitted to and from Discord. Please refer to Discord’s Privacy Policy.

Hosting Provider: The Bot is privately hosted on servers located in Germany. We have selected a provider that adheres to high security standards.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)
Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

Access: Ask for a copy of the data we hold about your User ID. You can also see a summary at any time yourself using the /data info command, which lists every category of data stored about you.

Rectification: Request correction of incorrect data.

Erasure: You can delete your own activity data at any time with the /data delete command in Discord. Moderation records (warnings and moderation actions) are kept when you do so, because a self-service deletion must not be usable to escape a server's moderation history; we retain them on the basis of legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). To request the erasure of those records as well, contact us using the details in Section 1 and we will review the request.

Restriction & Objection: Restrict how we process your data or object to processing based on legitimate interest.

Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, please contact us via the email address listed in Section 1 or join our Support Server.

7. Security
We implement appropriate technical measures (e.g., access controls, minimal data collection) to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
